KEY FINDINGS
------------

COSTA RICA:
- Strongest ecosystem with 22+ startups identified
- Strong biotech sector (therapeutics, biocontrol)
- Agtech focus on: precision agriculture, waste management, alternative proteins
- Key accelerators: AUGE-UCR, TEC Emprende Lab
- Major support: CINDE, CR BioMed cluster
- Recent venture: Lilliput Technologies (Deep Science Ventures spinout, 2024)
- Coverage: 19 AgriTech startups recorded (Tracxn)

PANAMA:
- Smaller startup ecosystem: 7 startups identified
- Tech/software focus more than biotech
- Innovation hub: Ciudad del Saber (120 hectare campus, 250+ orgs)
- Government support: SENACYT (National Science/Tech agency)
- Funding: $4M+ annually through Ciudad del Saber
- Note: Less biotech/agtech data available; ecosystem more fintech/software focused

GUATEMALA:
- Growing ecosystem: 14 startups identified
- Food/AgTech focus: 27 FoodTech startups (Tracxn)
- Notable successes: Kingo (solar, 2013), Genba (bakery platform, 2016), Wakami (artisans)
- Accelerator support: Potencia tu Agroempresa, VerdeXcelerate
- University presence: Campus TEC Guatemala
- Women-focused: Wakami (50+ women artisans), targets 40%+ women-led

REGIONAL INITIATIVES:
- VerdeXcelerate (2025-2026): 100+ startups, 3,000+ farmers, $4M+ investment
- Potencia tu Agroempresa: 39 accelerated, $71M sales growth, 50% women-led
- Countries covered: CR, PA, GT + Colombia, El Salvador, Honduras
