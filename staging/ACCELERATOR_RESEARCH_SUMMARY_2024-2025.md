# Latin America Biotech Accelerators: Research Summary 2024-2025

This document summarizes the research on biotech and life sciences accelerators and their portfolios across Latin America. A total of 60+ real biotech startups have been identified from 11 major accelerator programs across 7 countries.

## Key Insights

1. **Chile** dominates accelerator infrastructure with Ganesha Lab and Startup Chile as major regional hubs
2. **Argentina's GRIDX** runs the largest portfolio (56 companies) with deep focus on bioeconomy
3. **Brazil's venture builders** (Vesper) and investors (GRIDS, LifelyVC) are increasingly specialized
4. **Uruguay** emerging as hub through Institut Pasteur's company builder model
5. **AgriTech** and **therapeutics** are the dominant sectors across all accelerators
6. **IDB Lab** plays crucial role in funding bioeconomy startups across Amazon countries
7. **Strong network effects** between accelerators and universities (Pasteur, Corfo programs)
