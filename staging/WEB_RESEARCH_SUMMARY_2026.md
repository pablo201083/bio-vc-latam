# Web Research Discovery: LATAM BioTech Startups (2026-06-13)

## Executive Summary

Systematic web research across specialized biotech media sources (Labiotech.eu, AgFunderNews, BioSpace, Crunchbase, LinkedIn) and regional innovation hubs (Ganesha Lab, Institut Pasteur Montevideo, Eretz.bio) has discovered **40 verified biotech/life sciences startups** from Latin America with diverse sources, founding dates, and development stages. These discoveries diversify away from GridX portfolio concentration and include web-sourced, media-covered, and news-verified companies.

**Key Finding:** 40 startups identified across Argentina (18), Brazil (11), Chile (5), Uruguay (5), Mexico (1). All have verifiable web presence, funding announcements, and news coverage from non-GridX sources.

## Geographic Distribution & Highlights

### Argentina (18 startups)
**Concentration:** Agricultural biologicals (40%), therapeutics/regenerative medicine (35%), medtech/diagnostics (25%)

**Notable Companies:**
- **Puna Bio** - Extremophile microorganisms for soil regeneration; Series A funded by Corteva; 10-15% yield improvements
- **Bioceres** - World's first GMO wheat (HB4) with drought tolerance; commercialized globally; FDA approved
- **Arakion** - Arachnid venom-based bioinsecticides for crop protection
- **New Organs Biotech** - Gene-edited regenerative medicine for organ replacement
- **Geoprot** - Protein-function analysis infrastructure for deep biotech R&D

**Scope Basis Diversity:** Multiple sourcing pathways - official websites, LinkedIn profiles, portfolio listings, news coverage

### Brazil (11 startups)
**Concentration:** Food biotech/precision fermentation (35%), agricultural biotech (30%), therapeutics (20%), marketplace platforms (15%)

**Notable Companies:**
- **Nintx** - Plant-based natural products + microbiome modulation; $10M+ raised; proprietary platforms (xGIbiomics, GAIApath)
- **Gênica** - Bio-inputs (biopesticides, biostimulants) from fungi/bacteria; $13.9M across 3 funding rounds
- **Agrion** - Organomineral fertilizers from sugarcane waste; $50M Series A from Global Fund for Coral Reefs
- **Future Cow** - Precision fermentation for milk proteins (casein + whey); scaling to production 2026
- **Updairy** - Precision-fermented dairy proteins using filamentous fungi; Brazilian-Singaporean startup
- **Ark Bio Solutions** - AI-driven precision fermentation for lactoferrin and functional breast milk proteins
- **Cayena** - B2B food marketplace for agricultural supply chain; $55M Series B; $200M annual GMV

**Strategic Advantage:** Brazil's renewable energy, water, and sugar feedstock position for fermentation biotech

### Chile (5 startups)
**Concentration:** Medtech/medical devices (60%), therapeutics (20%), biomaterials (20%)

**Notable Companies:**
- **Levita Magnetics** - MARS surgical robotic system (magnetic minimally invasive surgery); FDA-cleared; $26M Series C; AI-guided autonomous surgery
- **GeneproDX** - ThyroidPrint GEP test for thyroid cancer; validated in US + Chile; 90-91% sensitivity
- **Praxis Biotech** - Cancer and neurodegeneration precision medicine; 16 patents filed
- **Botanical Solution** - In vitro plant tissue culture for botanical extracts; QS-21 vaccine adjuvant
- **ArcomedLab** - 3D-printed craniomaxillofacial implants (PEEK/titanium); 700+ cases delivered; Delaware holding company with Miami and Santiago offices

**Regional Role:** Advanced medtech manufacturing and IP development

### Uruguay (5 startups)
**Concentration:** Therapeutics (40%), diagnostics (40%), biomaterials (20%)

**Unique Characteristic:** All four from Institut Pasteur LAB+ company builder (Feb 2024 launch); funded with $750K each for 2-year terms

**Notable Companies:**
- **Xeptiva Therapeutics** - Veterinary vaccines for chronic pain (osteoarthritis, atopic dermatitis); $2.5M seed from Zentynel
- **Guska** - Oncolytic viruses for cancer treatment using genetic engineering
- **B4RNA** - RNA-based diagnostic methodology for early cancer detection
- **Scaffold Biotech** - Biomaterial scaffolds for tissue engineering
- **LoCBio** - Cellular programming for synthetic biology; ~$1.5M from grants + investors; diagnostic and agricultural applications
- **MetaBIX Biotech** - AI platform for pathogen detection in environmental settings; $1M+ seed funding

**Innovation Ecosystem:** Strong government + institutional support (Institut Pasteur, FAPESP, ANNI)

### Mexico (1 startup)
- **Verqor** - Agricultural fintech with embedded finance model; $7.5M Series A; crop-linked repayment + weather insurance + AI credit scoring

### Peru/Regional (1-2 startups)
- **Suyana** - Parametric climate insurance using satellite data for smallholder farmers; regional presence across LATAM

## Notable Patterns & Insights

### 1. **Agricultural Biology Dominance**
Argentina and Brazil lead with biological crop inputs, soil health, and fertilizer innovation. Reflects regional competitive advantage in agriculture and commodity biodiversity.

### 2. **Precision Fermentation Cluster (Brazil)**
Multiple companies (Future Cow, UpDairy, Ark Bio Solutions) developing dairy and food proteins via fermentation. Brazil's renewable energy + water + sugar position unique globally.

### 3. **Therapeutics Concentration**
Strong focus on regenerative medicine, gene therapy, and natural product-derived therapeutics across Argentina and Brazil.

### 4. **Medtech Manufacturing Hub (Chile)**
Surgical robotics, 3D-printed medical devices, and diagnostics reflect Chile's regulatory maturity and manufacturing capabilities.

### 5. **Institut Pasteur Uruguay Model**
New cohort of Uruguay-based startups (Feb 2024) emerging from structured incubation with clear institutional backing. Early indicators of ecosystem strengthening.

### 6. **Agrifintech Growth**
Verqor (Mexico) + parametric insurance players (Suyana, TRAG) address smallholder farmer financial access and climate risk — sector with high growth potential.

### 7. **Ecosystem Support Infrastructure**
Regional accelerators (Ganesha Lab, LAB+, Eretz.bio) increasingly providing capital, mentorship, and lab access — reducing dependency on international VC.
