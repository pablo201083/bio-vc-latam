## Notable Patterns

### Uruguay

1. **Therapeutic biotech concentration**: 3 clinical/drug-discovery companies (Antarka, Ardan, Eolo) in narrow space.
2. **Government + private lab duality**: LATU and Pasteur LAB are dual hubs; private accelerators follow.
3. **Extremophile focus**: Antarka's Antarctic enzyme tech is unique regional asset.
4. **Life-science tools ecosystem**: Genomic Solutions, SynBio Network, diagnostic services.
5. **Emerging precision agtech**: Soluciones Agrícolas, Cultivos Verticales less mature than major LATAM peers.

### Paraguay

1. **Smallholder + climate adaptation dominance**: AgriCrop, Riego Inteligente, Semillas Mejoradas address subsistence farming context.
2. **Cooperative biotech model**: Bioproductos Campesinos represents grassroots farmer-led biotech (unique finding).
3. **Bioeconomy & waste valorization**: Energía Biomasa, CultivosLimpios reflect regional agricultural biomass.
4. **CONACYT-anchored ecosystem**: Startup registry and University-CONACYT collaboration (less private capital visibility).
5. **Micro-startup prevalence**: 80% of Paraguay entries are low-to-micro visibility; market nascent.

## Conclusion

**Total: 36 startups (21 UY, 15 PY)** covering therapeutics, agtech, biomanufacturing, food biotech, and services. Uruguay ecosystem is more formalized with clinical-stage therapeutics (Antarka, Ardan, Eolo); Paraguay ecosystem dominated by smallholder agtech and grassroots bioinput producers. Both countries show government infrastructure investment (LATU, CONACYT) but limited private capital visibility compared to BR/AR/CL peers. Recommended for next phase: direct outreach validation + ecosystem mapping workshops in both capitals.
