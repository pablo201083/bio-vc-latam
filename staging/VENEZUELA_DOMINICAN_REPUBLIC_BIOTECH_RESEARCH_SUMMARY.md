## Key Findings

### Biotech Concentration
- **Dominican Republic leads** with genuine biotech (SOS Biotech + CRO/diagnostic services)
- **Venezuela's strength is in HealthTech/MedTech** (telemedicine, diagnostics software, AI)
- **Crossover: AgTech dominates both countries** (food supply, precision farming)

### Funding Landscape
- **Venezuela**: 5 funded Food/Ag startups (Tracxn). Largest: Yummy ($69M). 
  - HealthTech: 7 companies, $310K total VC (very early stage)
  - Decline: -54.8% company formation 2023→2024
- **Dominican Republic**: 54 funded companies, $334M total VC. Growing ecosystem (+52.9% in 2025)

### University Ecosystems
- **Venezuela**: UCV, USB, UNIMET, UCAB participation in Startup Summit 2025; UNU-BIOLAC collaboration
- **Dominican Republic**: PUCMM/UNIBE driving STEM entrepreneurship; WISE program active

### Economic Context
- **Venezuela**: Diaspora-driven (7M+ diaspora). Food/fintech solving remittance + supply-chain problems
- **Dominican Republic**: Nearshoring hub for medtech manufacturing (Medtronic, J&J, Edwards present). Growing startup ecosystem

### Gaps/Challenges
1. **Limited pure biotech startups** in Venezuela (HealthTech software, not wet biotech)
2. **AgTech data fragmentation** — most startups post-seed or early-stage; limited funding data
3. **Micro-ventures underrepresented** — databases focus on funded companies
4. **Scale/revenue data sparse** — many startups have no public funding rounds

### Secondary Sources (Medium Confidence)
- **LinkedIn**, **F6S**, **Crunchbase** — founder profiles, company info (sometimes outdated)
- **News/PR** — Yummy, PEGASI, LataMed AI (time-bound announcements)

### Low Coverage Areas
- **Micro-ventures** (<$500K revenue, pre-funded)
- **Academic spinoffs** (UCV, USB likely have more but not tracked in startup databases)
- **Diaspora biotech** (scattered across US, Spain, Chile)
