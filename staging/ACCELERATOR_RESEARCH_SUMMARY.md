# Latin American Biotech & Life Sciences Accelerator Portfolio Research
## June 2026 Research Summary

### Overview
Comprehensive research on biotech and life sciences accelerators across Latin America with public portfolios. Research identified 54 biotech startups from 7 countries across 6 major accelerator programs.

### Key Findings

#### Geographic Distribution
- **Argentina (AR)**: 10 startups
- **Brazil (BR)**: 11 startups  
- **Chile (CL)**: 33 startups
- **Colombia (CO)**: 3 startups
- **Mexico (MX)**: 6 startups
- **Uruguay (UY)**: 5 startups
- **Costa Rica**: Research indicates CATIE and Deep Science Ventures presence but limited published portfolio data

#### Accelerator Programs Identified

##### 1. **GridX / Grid Exponential** (Founded 2016)
- **Location**: Argentina (Buenos Aires)
- **Portfolio Size**: 81+ biotechnology startups across 7 countries
- **Focus**: Life science venture capital fund for biotech (non-therapeutics)
- **Investment Model**: $200k initial + up to $1.5M in Seed/Series A rounds
- **URL**: https://www.gridexponential.com
- **Sectors Covered**: Healthcare, agriculture, bioindustry, biomaterials
- **Notable Companies**: Stämm, Puna Bio, CASPR Biotech, OncoPrecision, Michroma, Bioeutectics, Beeflow, Nanogrow

##### 2. **Ganesha Lab** (Founded 2017)
- **Location**: Santiago, Chile
- **Portfolio Size**: 36+ portfolio companies extracted
- **Focus**: Global biotech scale-up for science and technology-based startups
- **Program**: 4-month acceleration in Chile + potential 1-2 months in USA
- **URL**: https://theganeshalab.com
- **Sectors Covered**: Biomedicine, agricultural innovation, medical technology, health diagnostics
- **Notable Companies**: TELL, Patagon Fiber, Nanotransfer, Eywa, Amplify Dynamics, Trears Biomarkers, metaBIX Biotech, Smart Tissues, Pannex

##### 3. **Yield Lab Latam** (Founded 2017)
- **Location**: Buenos Aires, Argentina
- **Portfolio Size**: 35+ companies (AgTech/AgriBiotech focus)
- **Focus**: AgriFoodTech venture capital specializing in food systems
- **URL**: https://theyieldlablatam.com/portfolio/
- **Sectors Covered**: Precision farming, agricultural fintech, food tech, sustainable agriculture
- **Notable Companies**: PhageLab, Symbiomics, BemAgro, Culttivo, CerradoX, Produzindo Certo, Verqor, Blooms Capital

##### 4. **Lab+ (Instituto Pasteur de Montevideo)** (Founded 2021)
- **Location**: Montevideo, Uruguay
- **Portfolio Size**: 4 companies (first cohort); $25M fund for 16 startups in 4 years
- **Focus**: Scientific-technological startup incubation
- **Funding**: $750k per company for 2-year term (first cohort)
- **URL**: https://labplus.uy
- **Sectors Covered**: Oncology, diagnostics, therapeutics, cell therapy
- **Companies**: B4RNA, Guska, Scaffold Biotech, LoCBio

##### 5. **Start-Up Chile / Corfo** (Founded 2010)
- **Location**: Santiago, Chile
- **Portfolio Size**: 1,800+ startups total; 2,200+ total supporters since 2010
- **Focus**: Government-backed business acceleration program
- **Portfolio Value**: $5.8B+ portfolio valuation
- **Programs**: Build ($15M), Ignite ($25M), Growth ($75M)
- **URL**: https://startupchile.org
- **Sectors Covered**: Health & Biotech (30.8% increase recent generation), agriculture, fintech, IT
- **Health/Biotech Notable**: Mycorium Biotech and 100+ other health startups across cohorts

##### 6. **500 Global** (Founded 2010)
- **Location**: Multiple offices including Montevideo, Uruguay
- **Program**: 500 Montevideo Accelerator (6-week program)
- **Portfolio**: 300+ regionally headquartered startups in Latin America
- **Focus**: Growth-stage mentorship and investor connections
- **URL**: https://500.co/latam
- **Notable**: metaBIX Biotech (Uruguay, environmental biotech)

##### 7. **Deep Science Ventures (TABI Initiative)**
- **Location**: Costa Rica partnership with CRUSA Foundation
- **Focus**: Tropical Agriculture & Bioeconomy Initiative
- **URL**: https://www.deepscienceventures.com
- **Sectors**: Thermal adaptation, plant breeding, biomass conversion, composite materials

##### 8. **IndieBio** (Founded 2014)
- **Location**: San Francisco, USA (with LATAM reach)
- **Portfolio**: 251+ startups globally; 3 unicorns; 7 exits (as of Feb 2026)
- **LATAM Activity**: Supported several GridX portfolio companies including Stämm, CASPR, Michroma, Puna Bio, OncoPrecision
- **URL**: https://indiebio.co
- **Focus**: Early-stage biotech acceleration

### Data Quality Observations

**Strengths:**
- Rich public portfolio data from major accelerators (GridX: 81 companies, Ganesha Lab: 36+)
- Clear acceleration program structures and funding models documented
- Founding years available for ~70% of identified companies
- Sector classifications consistent across sources

**Gaps:**
- Costa Rica and some Colombian accelerators lack detailed public portfolio lists
- Website URLs unavailable for ~15% of companies
- No standardized founding date format across sources
- Limited information on exit outcomes and funding rounds beyond initial acceleration

### Investment Trends

**2025-2026 Market Observations:**
- Argentina emerging as Latin America's biotech leader (GridX headquarters, GRID EXPONENTIAL 90+ investments)
- Brazil retains largest startup funding share (~49% of VC dollars)
- Biotech/deeptech solidifying as top vertical (43.1% of Argentine startup agreements in 2025)
- Government support: Corfo (Chile), IDB Lab (region-wide), Conacyt (Mexico)
- Corporate VC engagement: Kamay Ventures (Coca-Cola, Grupo Bimbo, Grupo Arcor backing)
