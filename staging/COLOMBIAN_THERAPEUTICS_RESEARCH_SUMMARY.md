### 3. **Amplify Dynamics** ⭐⭐⭐
**Country:** Colombia + US headquarters model  
**Sector:** Bioprocessing/Pharmaceutical therapeutics (enabling infrastructure)  
**Founded:** 2023  
**Website:** https://amplify-dynamics.com/  

**Description:**  
Colombian/US bioprocessing tools company developing **contactless ultrafiltration platform** for lipid nanoparticle (LNP) therapeutics manufacturing. Combines microfluidic flows with electronically controlled ultrasonic waves to separate LNPs without physical membranes.

**Key Technology:**  
- Membrane-free ultrasound ultrafiltration for LNP purification
- Increases particle recovery by reducing loss in traditional methods
- Decreases purification time and waste generation
- Enables scalable manufacturing for gene therapy, vaccines, and nanomedicines

**Research Backing:**  
- **Official website verification** (technical team bios indicate research-institutional origins)
- **Startup master dataset inclusion** (classified as core bioprocessing infrastructure)
- Addresses critical bottleneck in therapeutic manufacturing

**Therapeutic Applications:**  
- Gene therapy manufacturing
- mRNA vaccine production
- Lipid nanoparticle therapeutics for cancer, immunotherapy
- Nanomedicine delivery platforms

**Funding Status:** Seed-stage (founded 2023, 3 years operational)

**Competitive Advantage:** Only contactless LNP purification platform identified in LATAM; solves manufacturing bottleneck for therapeutic scale-up

**Source:** Official website + startup master dataset + quality assessment
