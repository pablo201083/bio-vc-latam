## Gap Analysis: Why Only 2 Were Found Initially

1. **Domain Tag Fragmentation:** Startups tagged with general `agri-food` or `climate-resource` without `biodiversity-nature` may not surface in nature-tech queries
2. **Emerging Sector:** Nature & Ecosystem Tech in Mexico is nascent; many startups are recent (2018-2020) and have limited English presence
3. **Geographic Data Quality:** Some multi-country operations (PlantVerd, Bioram, FieldFactors) may have primary HQ outside Mexico or subsidiary structure not captured
4. **Academic Spillouts:** Tec de Monterrey and UNAM spin-offs may not yet be formally tracked in commercial databases

## Sector Context: Why Mexico for Nature & Ecosystem Tech?

### Natural Advantages:
1. **Megadiverse Country:** #2 globally in biodiversity (after Indonesia)
2. **Agricultural Footprint:** 15% of global cropland; significant agribusiness sector
3. **Water Stress:** Northern Mexico water scarcity; opportunity for restoration
4. **Ecosystem Services Value:** Coral, mangrove, cloud forest, chinampa systems
5. **Supply Chain Demand:** Strong agricultural export sector; buyers increasingly demand regenerative/circular supply chains

### Recent Policy Signals:
- Heineken Mexico & Fundación Azteca ecosystem restoration agreement (2026)
- COBIOCOM (Central-Occident Biocultural Corridor) territorial implementation phase
- CONACYT biotech R&D investment initiatives
- Public-private partnerships (Tec-UNAM Pharma Unit model extensible to biotech)

### Investment Context:
- Regenera Ventures I: $4M fund focused on regenerative agriculture in Mexico (DFC backing)
- The Yield Lab LatAm actively investing in Mexico agribusiness
- Miller Center Capital catalytic funding for Tierra de Monte
- Global capital seeking climate/nature impact in emerging markets
